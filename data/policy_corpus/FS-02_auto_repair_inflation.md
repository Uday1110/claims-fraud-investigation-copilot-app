# FS-02 Auto Repair Estimate Inflation

## Overview
A repair facility inflates a genuine but minor loss by billing replaced parts that were repaired or never damaged,
adding labour hours, and filing supplemental estimates after the vehicle is released and inspection is impossible.

## Red-Flag Indicators
- RF-06 Claimed amount at least 1.8x the independent estimate.
- RF-07 Repair shop linked to several prior claims with supplements or SIU flags.
- RF-14 Evidence inconsistency, e.g. photos show an undamaged panel that was billed as replaced.
- Multiple supplemental estimates submitted after vehicle release.

## Correlation Pattern
Estimate gap (RF-06) combined with a shop pattern (RF-07) indicates build-up. When photo evidence contradicts the
invoice (RF-14) the pattern is confirmed at MEDIUM likelihood; it escalates to HIGH only when the claimant is
shown to be complicit (kick-backs, shared contacts).

## Investigation Steps
1. Commission an independent re-inspection or desk review of the photos against invoice line items.
2. Compare this shop's supplement rate against the market average.
3. Request parts invoices from the shop's suppliers.

## Recommended Action
MEDIUM likelihood: monitor the claim and place the repair facility on a watch list; pay only verified line items.
Refer to SIU if the shop pattern spans many claimants or the claimant is complicit.
