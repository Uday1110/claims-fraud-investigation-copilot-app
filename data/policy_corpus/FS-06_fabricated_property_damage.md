# FS-06 Pre-Existing or Fabricated Property Damage

## Overview
The insured buys a policy after damage has already occurred (for example a long-term leak) and then reports it as
a new sudden loss, sometimes with a cooperating restoration contractor who starts work before inspection.

## Red-Flag Indicators
- RF-01 Loss within 60 days of policy inception.
- RF-02 Late reporting (more than 14 days after the stated loss date).
- RF-14 Evidence inconsistency: photo metadata predating the policy, long-term mould inconsistent with a sudden leak.
- RF-15 No independent witnesses.

## Correlation Pattern
A new policy (RF-01) plus evidence that the damage predates inception (RF-14) is the core signature. Late reporting
(RF-02) and restoration work started before inspection strengthen it to HIGH likelihood.

## Investigation Steps
1. Extract and verify photo EXIF metadata; request original image files.
2. Commission a cause-and-duration assessment (mould growth age, corrosion).
3. Obtain prior-owner / home-inspection reports from before inception.

## Recommended Action
Refer to SIU (HIGH) when photo or physical evidence places the damage before inception. Human sign-off required
before any coverage decision.
