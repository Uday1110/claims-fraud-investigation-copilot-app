# FS-01 Staged Auto Collision

## Overview
Organised rings deliberately cause or fabricate low-speed rear-end collisions so that several occupants can file
soft-tissue bodily-injury claims. A "capper" recruits passengers, a cooperating attorney is retained almost
immediately, and treatment is steered to a single clinic that bills a standard, maximal treatment plan.

## Red-Flag Indicators
- RF-04 Multiple soft-tissue injury claimants (three or more) from a single low-speed impact.
- RF-03 Attorney retained within three days of the loss, often before first medical visit.
- RF-07 Clinic, body shop or attorney linked to several prior SIU-flagged claims.
- RF-05 No police report despite multiple injuries; parties "agreed" not to call police.
- RF-01 Policy incepted shortly before the loss.
- RF-17 Contact details (phone/address) shared with other claimants in the book.
- RF-06 Claimed amount far above the independent physical-damage estimate.

## Correlation Pattern
The scheme is indicated when injury volume (RF-04) co-occurs with early legal representation (RF-03) AND a linked
provider/attorney ring (RF-07). Any two of these plus a new policy (RF-01) or missing police report (RF-05) is a
strong staged-collision signature. The ring link is the anchor indicator.

## Investigation Steps
1. Pull prior claims for every occupant and search for shared phones, addresses, clinics and attorneys.
2. Request event data recorder / telematics download and scene photos.
3. Take recorded statements from all occupants separately; compare seating and trip purpose.
4. Request clinic treatment notes and compare treatment plans across the ring's patients.

## Recommended Action
Refer to SIU when the correlation pattern is present (fraud-likelihood HIGH). Referral requires human SIU sign-off;
the copilot must never deny the claim or withhold payment on its own.
