# FS-09 Post-Catastrophe Contractor and AOB Abuse

## Overview
After a catastrophe, door-to-door contractors solicit homeowners, obtain an assignment of benefits (AOB), and submit
grossly inflated estimates, often followed by litigation.

## Red-Flag Indicators
- RF-07 Contractor linked to multiple flagged or litigated claims from the same event.
- RF-06 Contractor estimate far above the independent adjuster estimate.
- RF-02 Late reporting well after the catastrophe date.
- AOB signed on the day of solicitation.

## Correlation Pattern
Contractor cluster (RF-07) plus estimate inflation (RF-06) on a late-reported catastrophe claim (RF-02) indicates
contractor-driven inflation at MEDIUM likelihood; the homeowner is usually not the primary actor.

## Investigation Steps
1. Obtain the AOB document and the solicitation timeline.
2. Commission an independent re-inspection and compare scopes line by line.
3. Check contractor licensing and complaint history.

## Recommended Action
MEDIUM: monitor, pay the undisputed independent estimate, and add the contractor to the watch list. Refer to SIU if
the contractor pattern is confirmed across claimants.
