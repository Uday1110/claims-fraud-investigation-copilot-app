# FS-07 Vehicle Owner Give-Up

## Overview
An owner who owes more on a vehicle than it is worth arranges for the vehicle to be "stolen" and destroyed
(often burned) to collect the insured value and clear the loan.

## Red-Flag Indicators
- RF-10 Financial distress: loan balance exceeds vehicle value, late premiums.
- RF-14 Evidence inconsistency: owner still holds all keys; vehicle recovered burned or stripped nearby.
- RF-02 Delayed theft report.
- RF-15 No witnesses.

## Correlation Pattern
Negative equity (RF-10) with all keys accounted for and a burned recovery (RF-14) is a HIGH-likelihood give-up
pattern, particularly when the theft report is delayed (RF-02).

## Investigation Steps
1. Obtain the loan payoff and payment history.
2. Have a forensic locksmith examine the ignition/immobiliser and the keys.
3. Obtain the police theft report and recovery report; check telematics.

## Recommended Action
Refer to SIU (HIGH). Human sign-off required; the copilot must not deny the theft claim.
