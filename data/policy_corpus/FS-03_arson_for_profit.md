# FS-03 Arson for Profit

## Overview
The insured deliberately sets, or arranges, a fire to collect on a policy, usually under financial pressure.
Valuable contents are frequently removed before the fire, and coverage limits are raised shortly before the loss.

## Red-Flag Indicators
- RF-09 Coverage increased materially (20%+) within 90 days before the loss.
- RF-10 Financial distress: repeated late premium payments, arrears, loan pressure.
- RF-14 Evidence inconsistency: accelerant patterns, contents moved out before the fire, insured conveniently away.
- RF-18 Large loss (claimed amount at or above 100,000).

## Correlation Pattern
A recent coverage increase (RF-09) together with financial distress (RF-10) gives motive and preparation; physical
evidence (accelerant, removed contents; RF-14) gives means. Motive plus means is a HIGH-likelihood arson pattern.

## Investigation Steps
1. Obtain the fire marshal / cause-and-origin report and laboratory accelerant results.
2. Obtain financial records (mortgage arrears, liens) with appropriate legal basis.
3. Canvass neighbours about removal of contents; check storage-unit rentals.
4. Take an examination under oath where the policy allows it.

## Recommended Action
Refer to SIU (HIGH). Coordinate with the fire marshal. Human sign-off required before any coverage position is taken.
