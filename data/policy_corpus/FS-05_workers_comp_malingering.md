# FS-05 Workers Compensation Malingering

## Overview
An employee reports an injury that occurred outside work (for example during weekend sport) as a workplace injury,
or exaggerates the duration and severity of a genuine injury to extend benefits.

## Red-Flag Indicators
- RF-12 Unwitnessed injury, classically first thing on a Monday morning.
- RF-08 Frequent claimant: prior similar claims in the last 36 months.
- RF-14 Evidence inconsistency, e.g. social-media activity incompatible with the claimed injury.

## Correlation Pattern
Unwitnessed Monday-morning injury (RF-12) plus contradicting activity evidence (RF-14) indicates a possible
non-occupational injury. With prior similar claims (RF-08) likelihood is MEDIUM; HIGH requires surveillance or
medical evidence contradicting the claim.

## Investigation Steps
1. Obtain the employer's first report and shift records.
2. Review the treating provider's notes for mechanism-of-injury consistency.
3. Consider a lawful activity check; preserve public social-media evidence.

## Recommended Action
MEDIUM: monitor, request an independent medical examination, and diary a review in 30 days.
Refer to SIU only if activity evidence is corroborated.
